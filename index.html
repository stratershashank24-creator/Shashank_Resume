About

From fading channels to production code

I trained as an Electronics & Communication Engineer at ACE Engineering College in Ghatkesar — a JNTUH-affiliated college (now autonomous) — where my final-year work looked at how signals hold up under noise and multipath interference.

That question turned out to be good preparation for a career in quality engineering. I joined Cognizant in October 2016 as a fresher and spent close to six years there, starting on a banking & investment project and later moving into healthcare, where I worked as a tester and grew into automation. Along the way I picked up the tools of the trade: Selenium, Cucumber, and the enterprise platforms that keep large systems observable and supportable.

Since July 2022, I've been a Senior Software Engineer at Optum Technology Solutions, carrying that same instinct for finding what's broken and reliability into building the software itself.
